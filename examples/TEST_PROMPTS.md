# PBR Studio – Test Prompts & Sample Files

Everything in this folder was tested on an 8 GB laptop GPU (RTX 5060) with SDXL base 1.0 and PBR Studio v2.1.
Use it to check your install in the first 5 minutes, then swap in your own materials.

---

## 1. Workflow 01 – Text to PBR (one material)

Paste one prompt into **Positive Prompt (change the MATERIAL)**. Keep the negative prompt that ships with the workflow.
Set **surface** in the *Height Map* node as listed (it controls how much 3D relief the maps get).

Prompt pattern (already in the workflow – only change the part in brackets):

```
photograph of [MATERIAL], seamless tileable PBR texture scan, top-down orthographic view, flat overcast diffuse lighting, no shadows, photorealistic real-world material, natural imperfections, high detail, sharp focus, 8k
```

| # | [MATERIAL] | Set surface to | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | weathered red brick wall with grey mortar | rough | facades, ArchViz exteriors |
| 2 | mossy cobblestone pavement | rough | streets, courtyards, game levels |
| 3 | travertine stone wall | rough | Mediterranean / villa walls |
| 4 | rough grey concrete with small pits | rough | brutalist walls, floors |
| 5 | dry cracked desert soil | rough | terrain, landscapes |
| 6 | asphalt with fine gravel | rough | roads, parking |
| 7 | grey granite floor tiles | rough | outdoor paving, plazas |
| 8 | rusty corroded metal plate | rough | industrial props (Metallic: bright_areas_only) |
| 9 | dark oak wood planks | flat | floors, furniture |
| 10 | light birch plywood | flat | interiors, joinery |
| 11 | weathered teak decking boards | flat | decks, terraces |
| 12 | beige linen fabric weave | flat | upholstery, curtains |
| 13 | rough off-white plaster wall | flat | interior walls |
| 14 | white marble with grey veins | polished | countertops, floors |

**Tips**
- Add a colour or age word to vary results: *"old"*, *"wet"*, *"sun-bleached"*, *"dark grey"*.
- Change the **seed** for a new variation of the same material.
- Leather, terracotta roof tiles and clay ground did **not** look realistic with SDXL base in our tests – avoid them or use a photo (workflow 02).
